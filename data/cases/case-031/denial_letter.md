Dear Megan Ivanova:

This notice concerns the request for coverage of ferric carboxymaltose intravenous infusion, billed under HCPCS code J1439, administered by Harborview Infusion Center, a network provider, for a member of the Halcyon Ridge Silver PPO. Halcyon Ridge Health has determined that the service is denied.

The denial is based on Section 6.1 of the member's plan, which requires prior authorization for specialty infused drugs. Ferric carboxymaltose IV infusion is a specialty infused drug and is subject to that requirement. The provider did not obtain prior authorization before the service was administered.

Because the required prior authorization was not obtained, the infusion is not covered under the member's plan. This determination is limited to the service identified above and is based solely on the prior authorization requirement in Section 6.1.

**Your right to appeal.** You have the right to appeal this decision. See the Appeals section of your plan's Evidence of Coverage for how and when to file. If waiting for a standard review could seriously harm your health, you may ask for an expedited appeal.
