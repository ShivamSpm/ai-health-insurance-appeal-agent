Dear Hector Larsen:

This notice confirms that Halcyon Ridge Health has denied coverage for the spinal cord stimulator trial, CPT 63650, requested by Dr. Omar Siddiqui of Valley Pain Management for your Halcyon Ridge Bronze HMO plan.

The request is denied because the spinal cord stimulator trial is not medically necessary. Under Section 5.2 of your plan, coverage requires documentation that conservative treatment has failed for at least six weeks. The records submitted do not document at least six weeks of failed conservative treatment.

Accordingly, the requested service does not meet the medical necessity criteria in Section 5.2, and no benefits are available for this service.

**Your right to appeal.** You have the right to appeal this decision. See the Appeals section of your plan's Evidence of Coverage for how and when to file. You must file any appeal within 30 days of the date of this letter. If waiting for a standard review could seriously harm your health, you may ask for an expedited appeal.
