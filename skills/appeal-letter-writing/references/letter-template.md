# Appeal letter template

Replace everything in angle brackets. Keep square-bracket placeholders for anything you don't know.

```
<Member full name>
[Your street address]
[City, State ZIP]
[Your phone number]

[Today's date]

<Insurer> Appeals Unit
<Appeals address from the plan, if retrieved> [cite]

Re: Appeal of adverse benefit determination
Member ID: <member ID>
Plan: <plan name>
Claim number: <claim number>
Provider: <provider>
Date of service: <date of service>
Service: <service and code>

Dear Appeals Reviewer:

I am appealing your decision dated <notice date> to deny <service>. <One sentence on why the decision should be reversed.>

<Argument paragraph 1: the facts, then the plan rule that applies [cite].>

<Argument paragraph 2, if needed. For an ambiguous denial, one paragraph per possible reason.>

<If the letter contradicts the plan: "The notice states <X>. However, my plan provides <Y> [cite].">

I ask that you <specific request>. <If urgent: "Because delay could seriously harm my health, I ask that this appeal be expedited [cite].">

Enclosed:
- <Documents the member has>
- [To be added: <documents still to gather>]

Sincerely,
<Member full name>
```
